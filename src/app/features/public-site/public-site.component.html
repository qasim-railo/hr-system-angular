Questions, answered

What is PeopleOS?

PeopleOS is a connected HR platform for employee records, payroll, attendance, leave, documents, assets and reporting.

Is PeopleOS suitable for Qatar?

Yes. PeopleOS starts with Qatar-ready defaults while keeping country, currency and time zone configuration extensible.

How quickly can we get started?

Register your workspace in minutes, invite your team, and continue setup at your own pace.

Can I speak with someone?

Absolutely. Our team can help you understand the right starting point for your organization.
